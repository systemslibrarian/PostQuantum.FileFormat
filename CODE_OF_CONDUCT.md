# Code of Conduct

PQF follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: the GitHub repository,
issues, pull requests, discussions, security advisories, and any other
project-affiliated communication.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and backgrounds.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback. Spec reviews and
  cryptographic critique are welcome — focus on the construction, not the author.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, deliberate misdirection, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to **systemslibrarian@gmail.com**. Reports will
be reviewed and acted on within a reasonable timeframe. Project maintainers
may remove comments, close issues, block contributors, or take other
proportionate action.

Confidentiality of reporters will be respected.
